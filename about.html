About Us

Fifteen Years of Building on Trust

Aurelia Estates designs and constructs residential and commercial developments across twelve Indian cities — guided by one idea: a building should serve the people inside it for decades, not just look good on launch day.

Company Story

From One Site to Twelve Cities

Aurelia Estates was founded in 2010 by a small team of architects and civil engineers who were frustrated by how few developers treated construction as a craft rather than a transaction. Our first project, a 40-unit apartment block in Whitefield, was built with the same site discipline we still use today — daily quality checks, documented material sourcing, and an open-door policy for buyers to visit during construction.

That approach scaled slowly and deliberately. Today we operate across Bengaluru, Mumbai, Pune, Chennai, Hyderabad and Noida, with more than 5,000 families and several hundred businesses now calling an Aurelia address home.

Architecture Philosophy

Designed to Age Well

We design for the way people actually live — cross-ventilation over decorative facades, generous storage over showpiece foyers, and material palettes chosen for how they'll look in year fifteen, not just at handover. Every layout is reviewed by our in-house design panel before it reaches a single brochure.

Quality Commitment

Verified at Every Stage

Independent structural audits, RERA registration on every project, and a dedicated quality team that inspects each unit before handover — quality at Aurelia is a documented process, not a marketing line.
